The Impact

Understanding The Problem & Our Solution

Over 80% of recyclable materials end up in landfills due to confusion over sorting rules and fragmented pickup logistics.

The Problem

  • Sorting Confusion: Residents are often unsure which bin items belong to (e.g., E-waste, mixed plastics, broken glass).
  • No Reliable Pickup Scheduling: Bulk recyclables and e-waste pile up in homes because regular trash trucks do not accept them.
  • Zero Operational Visibility: Collection crews struggle with manual logs and lack centralized status tracking.

The EcoCollect Solution

  • Instant Smart Guide: Type any item name to immediately see its category, proper bin color, and disposal tips.
  • Streamlined Doorstep Pickup: Book pickups in under 60 seconds with unique tracking IDs (e.g. EC-1001).
  • Unified Admin Dispatch & Metrics: Live dashboard to filter, assign, update progress, and visualize recovery statistics.
